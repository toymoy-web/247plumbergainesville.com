What to say when you call at night

The dispatcher needs four things: your address, what is happening, whether the water is off and whether anyone is at risk. If a toilet is overflowing, a main is backed up or water is coming through a ceiling, say so first.

If you are reaching us about a quote for a repair, mention the fixture or pipe and the age of the house. That lets us bring the right parts.
