Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 24, 2026. This policy explains what information this website and phone line collect and how it's used.

Information we collect

This site has no contact forms and doesn't ask you to create an account. We don't collect names, addresses or payment details through the website.

When you call the number on this site, the call is routed through a call-tracking service. That service may record basic call details such as your phone number, the time and length of the call, and the general area it came from. Calls may be recorded for quality and training purposes.

Like most websites, our hosting provider may log standard technical information such as IP address, browser type and pages visited, for security and performance.

Third-party services

Pages load fonts from Google Fonts and show maps through embedded Google Maps. Those services may collect information under their own privacy policies when the page loads.

When you call, your call may be answered by an independent local plumbing provider. Information you share on the call, such as your address, is used by that provider to schedule and perform the work.

How information is used

Call details are used to connect you with a provider, measure how the site performs and improve service. We don't sell personal information.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to contact@247plumbergainesville.com.
