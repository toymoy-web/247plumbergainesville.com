Drain Cleaning and Unclogging in Gainesville, FL

Drain cleaning clears a blockage from a sink, tub, toilet or the main sewer line so water flows again. The quick fixes are cable machines and augers. The stubborn ones, like grease, roots and scale, sometimes need water jetting and a camera to see what's left.

A single slow sink can wait for a daytime visit. Sewage coming up in a tub cannot, so emergency drain cleaning runs 24/7 across Gainesville.

  • Cleared from a cleanout where possible, not through the toilet.

What clogs Gainesville drains

Kitchen grease is the most common cause citywide. It goes down warm and liquid, then cools and coats the pipe a little further along. Every meal adds another layer until the line closes.

In the older neighborhoods, roots are the bigger problem. Big oaks shade the streets of the Duckpond, Pleasant Street and University Heights, and their roots find the joints in old clay and cast iron sewer laterals. A root mass can fill a four-inch pipe and come back within months of a basic cabling.

Cast iron drains in pre-1970 homes also scale and rust on the inside. The rough surface catches paper and grease, so the pipe clogs more often even when nothing unusual goes down it.

How we clear the line

  1. 1
    Find the right access point. Most homes have a sewer cleanout outside, often a capped pipe near the foundation. Working from there keeps the mess out of the house.
  2. 2
    Cable the blockage. A drain machine sized to the pipe cuts through the clog and restores flow.
  3. 3
    Test with volume. We run water through several fixtures at once to be sure the line carries a full load, not just a trickle.
  4. 4
    Camera if it keeps coming back. A second clog in a short span usually means roots, a sag or a break. A professional sewer camera inspection shows which.

Grease and roots often need more than a cable. Full-bore hydro jetting scours the pipe wall clean, so the line stays open longer.

Your line or the utility's?

If your neighbors are backing up at the same time you are, the problem may be in the public sewer main, not your pipe. In that case, call GRU first. It costs you nothing to check.

If it is only your house, the blockage is almost always in your own lateral, the pipe between the house and the utility connection. Clearing that line is plumbing work. If the camera shows a crack or a collapsed section there, sewer line repair is the next step.

While you wait for the truck

  • ✓
    Stop running water everywhere. No flushing, showers, laundry or dishwasher until the line is clear.
  • ✓
    Don't pour chemical drain cleaner into a full backup. It rarely clears a main line, and it makes the water dangerous for whoever opens the pipe.
  • ✓
    Move towels and rugs from around floor drains and tubs. That is where sewage shows up first.
  • ✓
    Find the outdoor cleanout if you can. Pointing us to it saves time. Leave the cap on if water is pushing against it.

Drains on septic outside the city

Not every backup is a clog. On homes with a septic tank, a full tank or a failing drain field backs up the same way a blocked main does. That is common on rural lots around High Springs and the rest of the county's unsewered areas.

If the tank has not been pumped in years, cabling the line only buys a few days. Routine septic service deals with the actual cause.

Clog behavior and cleanout access follow standard drainage practice under the Florida Building Code, Plumbing, based on the International Plumbing Code (IPC).
