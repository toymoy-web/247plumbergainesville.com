Clay ground, a different kind of slab problem

West of Gainesville, slab leaks trace back to shallow limestone and settling sand. Out east toward Hawthorne the ground is different. A thicker layer of clay sits over the limestone. That clay makes large sinkholes less likely, but it swells when it's wet and shrinks when it's dry.

North Central Florida swings between soaking summer storms and dry spells, so that clay moves a little every year. A slab moves with it, and so do the copper lines and drains inside it. Over decades, a joint or a thin spot gives way.

If you notice a warm spot on the floor, a hissing sound with every tap off, or cracks that are new, slab leak detection and repair finds the leak electronically before anything is cut.

Hawthorne's city water and sewer

The City of Hawthorne runs its own water and sewer system. Its wastewater plant on SE 65th Avenue has a capacity of 150,000 gallons a day, and residents within 200 feet of a sewer line are required to connect.

Connecting a home that was on septic means running a new line from the house to the sewer and abandoning the old tank properly. After connection, the lateral is the owner's to maintain, and main drain cleaning handles the clogs that follow.

City water mains break now and then, like any system. The city has issued precautionary boil notices for neighborhoods such as Ashley Oaks and The Reservation after a broken line. When service comes back, run cold taps first to clear air and sediment.

Active leak in Hawthorne? What to do before we arrive

Clay ground and older slabs shape how leaks show up in Hawthorne. These steps work whether the water is spraying or seeping.

1

Close the main valve.

Hawthorne homes on city water have a shutoff at the meter and often a second one where the line enters the slab or crawlspace. Close whichever is closer, then open a low faucet to drain the pressure.

2

Switch off power to wet rooms.

Trip the breakers for any room where water is near outlets. A slab leak can wet a floor near an outlet before you see a puddle, so treat damp baseboards the same way.

3

Dry the floor and mark the cracks.

Pull rugs and furniture back. If water is coming up through a floor crack, mark it with a pencil and note the time. That helps tell a supply leak from ground moisture.

4

Dial the emergency line.
