Where Suburban Heights is

Suburban Heights is in the northwest part of Gainesville. A 2021 local news story about a nearby development described its residents' concerns about traffic at NW 43rd Street and NW 23rd Avenue. The names on listings cluster along NW 12th to NW 20th Avenues and Places.

No source we found gives a formal boundary, so we describe the area by those streets and not by a fixed line.

Homes from the 1960s and later

Listing records show homes in Suburban Heights built from 1964 on, with a median near 1969 among sales since 1999. That is an age when owners often have replaced water heaters, toilet parts and faucets at least once.

We don't assume what any single house contains. What we can say is that houses of this age are at the stage where scheduled replacements pay off. See water heater repair and water heater installation for the common one.

A once a year check

  • ✓
    Flush the water heater. Draining some water from the tank each year helps clear sediment.
  • ✓
    Look under every sink. Check for drips, corrosion or damp cabinets.
  • ✓
    Test the toilets. A drop of food coloring in the tank shows a silent leak into the bowl.
  • ✓
    Read your meter. Turn off every fixture and see if the meter still moves.

If the meter moves with everything off, leak detection finds the source.

Repair or replace a water heater

A repair makes sense when the unit is fairly young and the fault is a single part, such as an element, a thermostat or a valve. Replacement makes sense when the tank itself is leaking, when repairs keep recurring or when the heater is near the end of its usual life.

Ask for the unit's age and the cost of each option before deciding, and ask whether a different size would suit the household better. A heater that was right for two people may not be right for five.
